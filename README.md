# Hi, I'm Gonzalo García Martínez-Echevarría

**Mathematical Engineering & Artificial Intelligence Student | Machine Learning | Quantitative Finance | NLP | Optimization**

I am studying Mathematical Engineering and Artificial Intelligence at ICAI, with a strong interest in quantitative finance, machine learning, statistical modeling, and optimization.

My work focuses on building applied AI systems: from NLP pipelines and computer vision projects to predictive models, optimization tools, and data-driven decision-making systems. I am especially interested in the use of machine learning and mathematical modeling for financial markets, portfolio construction, and quantitative research.
